What learners will accomplish

From AI-curious to confident and capable.

This course focuses on informed decision-making and practical application — not technical jargon or abstract theory.

The learning path

Six modules that build toward action.

Complete modules in sequence to move from understanding AI to creating an implementation plan that fits your business.

How the course works

Learning that leads to useful work.

Each lesson uses a repeatable experience designed to help learners apply what they are learning immediately.

  1. 01

    Follow a live demonstration

    Use a real AI tool and clear, copy-ready prompts to understand the process.

  2. 02

    Try it in your own context

    Complete a short activity that creates a usable business output, rather than a theoretical answer.

  3. 03

    Adapt the workflow

    Use the included diagrams, audio walkthroughs, and customization guidance to tailor the approach.

  4. 04

    Reflect and act

    Connect the lesson to your organization and identify one concrete next step before moving on.
